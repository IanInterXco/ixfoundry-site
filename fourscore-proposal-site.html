How we’d build it, together

Three phases, each with a clear handoff. Discovery is a fixed fee; the build and acceptance phases are priced as a single engagement once scope is settled.

Time-sensitive This proposal expires on May 15, 2026.
01 Fixed fee

Discovery & Design

We sit with Stephanie and the team, walk through the process as it stands today, and come back with a re-architected workflow shaped around the portal.

What we do
  • Interviews with Stephanie, Jesse, and the attorneys
  • Detailed mapping of intake, Clio, Faster Suite, and Xodo Sign touchpoints
  • Re-architected process design
  • Proposal of AI agents where they earn their place
  • Review of security and privacy requirements
  • Roadmap for future improvements beyond Phase 1
You receive A workflow design, agent recommendations, and a future roadmap
02 — 03 Scope-based fee — combined

Build

We build the portal iteratively. Stephanie and the team see progress along the way and steer us before anything is set in concrete.

What we do
  • Iterative implementation against the agreed design
  • Regular check-ins with Stephanie and the team
  • Course corrections as the team sees the build take shape
  • Integrations with Clio, Faster Suite, Xodo Sign, ActiveCampaign, and SoS lookups
  • Implementation of the AI agents agreed in Phase 01
  • Built to Fourscore’s security specifications
You receive A working portal, refined through your feedback at every step

Acceptance Testing

Stephanie, Fourscore, and our team test the platform end-to-end against the specification agreed in Phase 1.

What we do
  • Joint UAT with real intake scenarios
  • Defect resolution against agreed criteria
  • Training and handover to Stephanie
  • Sign-off and cutover from current process
